## Map Overview

This Wardley Map depicts the evolution of energy trading systems, focusing on the transition from traditional utility-based models to peer-to-peer (P2P) energy trading enabled by blockchain and IoT technologies.

**Anchor:** The anchor is the Energy Market, representing the ultimate user need for energy trading and consumption. Its high visibility and low evolution position indicate it's a well-established concept but still evolving in terms of how it operates.

### Consumer

- **Position:** High visibility, high evolution

- **Position:** High visibility, mid-high evolution
- **Evolution Stage:** Product
- **Strategic Significance:** Key to P2P energy trading, representing users who both consume and produce energy. Critical for the success of decentralized energy systems.

### Blockchain Platform

### IoT Devices

### Regulatory Framework
