## Map Overview

This Wardley Map illustrates HMRC's digital transformation efforts, focusing on modernizing tax administration and dispute resolution processes. It shows the evolution from traditional systems to digital platforms and services, highlighting the shift towards more integrated, data-driven, and user-centric approaches.

**Anchor:** The anchor (user need) is represented by 'Taxpayers' and 'Tax Professionals' at the top of the map. Their position indicates that they are the primary users of HMRC's services, and their needs drive the entire value chain. The significance of this anchor is that it emphasizes the importance of user-centric design in HMRC's digital transformation efforts.

### Real-time Information Processing

- **Position:** Lower mid-chain, evolving towards Product

### Data Analytics

- **Position:** Lower chain, early Product stage
